Who we are

A bridge between young volunteers and the communities of Mathare

DBSA was founded by university students in 2014 and has grown into an international volunteer organisation with full-time local teams in Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania. Our mission is youth empowerment in underserved regions — working on the ground, together with local teachers, headteachers and families.

In Mathare, one of Nairobi's largest informal settlements, we run year-round programmes spanning school renovation, daily school meals, learning resources and community culture.

Since 2014 we have rebuilt and equipped seven primary schools — each with a library — serving more than 2,000 pupils, and our Free Lunch International programme has reached 37 schools and 35,000 children across six countries, helping thousands return to the classroom.

Every programme is delivered with published budgets, competitive local procurement and signed handover agreements with each school.
