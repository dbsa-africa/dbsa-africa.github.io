How it works

Not a donation drop-off — a reading system

Books only change lives when they are read, term after term. Every Book Corner runs on the same four-part routine, agreed with each school in a signed donation agreement.

Curated, bilingual libraries

Each collection is built from the school's own wishlist: graded readers, English and Kiswahili storybooks, science and reference titles — matched to the grades the school teaches.

Teacher-managed bookcases

Books live in a lockable bookcase looked after by a designated teacher, keeping the collection organised, safe and accounted for.

Structured reading classes

During reading class the teacher issues books to every pupil, and collects and locks them back after the lesson — so books stay in circulation and in good condition.

Reporting & restocking

Schools log lost or damaged copies by scanning the QR code on their bookcase. DBSA reviews the records and restocks on follow-up visits.

  • Delivery & setup5%

Competitive procurement. Every purchase is quoted by at least three Nairobi booksellers, compared line by line, and settled only on delivery and inspection.

Reading books, not curriculum stock. Funds go to storybooks, knowledge and reference titles chosen from each school's wishlist — resources that outlast any single school year.

Signed and audited. Each school signs a donation agreement with a full book list attached; receipts, quotations and delivery records are kept for our funding partners, TECNO and the Amity Foundation.

For partner schools — report a lost or damaged book

Scan the QR code inside your bookcase door — it opens a one-minute form with your school already selected. Books can be reported lost, damaged, or found again, and every report helps us plan restocking.
