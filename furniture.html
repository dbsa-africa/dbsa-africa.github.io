Africa Dream Campus Project

A desk and a seat for every pupil

Across Mathare, lessons often happen on benches, floors and shared stools. Since 2024 DBSA has equipped 23 partner schools with steel-frame three-seater desks, blackboards and chairs — built to last, delivered to the classroom door.

How it works

From needs list to classroom, with a warranty

Furniture is agreed school by school, bought through competitive local procurement, and delivered against a signed order — then tracked for repairs long after the handover photo.

School-by-school needs list

Headteachers tell us what their classrooms are missing — desks, blackboards or chairs by size — and quantities are agreed together during school visits.

Competitive procurement

Orders go to Nairobi workshops on formal purchase orders with quality specifications, staged payment, and delivery included to each school.

Guaranteed quality

Suppliers repair or replace any defective item at no cost. Each delivery is received and signed for by the school's focal teacher.

Reporting & repairs

Schools report broken or missing furniture by scanning their QR code, and DBSA plans repairs and replacements on follow-up visits.

Transparency

Bought openly, delivered accountably

Formal purchase orders. Every batch is ordered on a written PO with quality specifications and staged payment — the balance is only released after the goods arrive and are accepted.

Delivery to the school door. Each school's items are listed on the order with a named focal teacher who receives and signs for the delivery.

Warranty on every item. Suppliers guarantee their work and repair or replace defects at no additional cost.

Audited end to end. Purchase orders, receipts and delivery records are kept for our funding partners, TECNO and the Amity Foundation.

For partner schools — report broken or missing furniture

Scan the furniture QR code posted in your school — it opens a one-minute form with your school already selected. Desks, blackboards and chairs can be reported damaged, lost, or repaired.
